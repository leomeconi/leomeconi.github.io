About

Leo Meconi is an Italian singer-songwriter blending American rock ’n’ roll roots with modern pop and folk influences. Born in Bologna in 2004, he grew up on a steady mix of Elvis Presley, Bob Dylan, Johnny Cash, and Bruce Springsteen. He started writing songs at a young age, moving from his bedroom to stages across Italy, playing everything from small bars and clubs to bigger venues and festivals. Over the years, he’s opened for major Italian artists including Sangiovanni and Vasco Rossi, performing at Bologna’s Dall’Ara Stadium in the summer of 2025. His music moves between loud, guitar-driven rock and more intimate, stripped-back songwriting, always keeping storytelling at its core. After releasing his first two albums and appearing on X Factor Italy in 2020, Leo teamed up with Irish multi-platinum producer and songwriter Don Mescall to record his third album, Heaven (November 2024), in Ireland. Now based in Boston, where he attends Berklee College of Music, Leo is working on new music, exploring a sound that brings together his rock ’n’ roll background with a more modern, alternative pop. More to come.
